Nimbus Academy of Management

Pool Campus Placement Drive Report - July 6, 2024

Event Overview

On July 6, 2024, Nimbus Academy of Management successfully hosted a one-day pool campus placement drive. The event saw enthusiastic participation from some of the leading companies in the industry and a significant turnout from students across various educational institutions in Dehradun, Roorkee, Rishikesh, and Haridwar

Participating Companies

The placement drive attracted top companies, including Paytm, HIKE EDU, GENIUS,SUCCESSPACT,HIGH RISE.CARD EXPERTISE.

Student Participation

The event drew over 250 applicants from a diverse array of universities, colleges, and institutes.

Registration and Selection Process

Candidates registered for the placement drive through both online and offline modes. The companies conducted a rigorous selection process that included:

  • Aptitude Tests
  • Group Discussions
  • Technical Interviews
  • HR Interviews

Placement Outcomes

The placement drive concluded on a successful note with 55 students receiving job offers. Highlighting the success, Aastha Mishra stood out by securing a position with an impressive package of 7 LPA.

Notable Placements

  • Aastha Mishra - 7 LPA package
  • Ekta Yadav -6 LPA package

Chairman's Remarks

Dr. Rajesh Kumar Tiwari, Chairman of Nimbus Academy of Management, expressed his heartfelt congratulations to all the selected students. He praised their hard work and dedication and wished them a prosperous future. Dr. Tiwari also thanked the participating companies for their support and highlighted the importance of such collaborations in bridging the gap between academia and industry.

Impact and Future Prospects

The Nimbus Academy of Management’s pool campus placement drive on July 6, 2024, stands as a significant milestone, demonstrating the institution's commitment to student success and industry engagement. The event's success underscores the robust academic foundation and high employability of the students from the participating institutions.
